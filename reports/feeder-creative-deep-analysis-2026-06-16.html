The core principle: a Feeder creative is NOT a Sales creative

A Conversion (Sales) campaign needs a closer: a creative that converts (high ROAS, strong hook).
A Feeder (Traffic) campaign needs an opener: a creative that delivers the most qualified visits per riyal (cheap CPC, high Landing-Page-Views) so it floods the pixel + warm pool that the Sales/Retargeting campaign then harvests.
➜ Therefore the Feeder is selected on cost-of-traffic, NOT on ROAS. Judging a feeder creative by its own ROAS is the exact mistake that got the cheap-reach creative («#68», «Wasayef-V2-C2») wrongly flagged as junk.

The decision model: Openers vs Closers (a 2×2)

Split every creative on two axes — cost of traffic (CPC) and conversion (ROAS). The role is decided by where it lands:

Cheap traffic + Converts VERSATILE
Ideal feeder primary — fills the pool and sells.
Zain: #69C1 · Noura: Asayel New V2 C1, Image carousel 3
Expensive traffic + Converts CLOSER → SALES
Keep in the Sales/RT campaign. Wasting these in a feeder burns their conversion power on cold cheap clicks.
Zain: #fypシ ✨, #fyp ❤️_#52 · Noura: وصايف تجنن, نجلاء, carousel 4
Cheap traffic + Low ROAS OPENER → FEEDER
The feeder workhorse. Low ROAS is irrelevant here — its job is cheap reach.
Zain: #54-C1-OW, #103-C1-OW · Noura: Wasayef-V2-C2
Expensive traffic + Low ROAS KILL / relaunch
No role. Kill or rebuild with a new angle.
e.g. #50 C2-OU, الرد على @M variants

Method & sources (so you can audit every call)

Data

  • Source: Windsor.ai → TikTok connector, ad-level.
  • Accounts: Zain 7494645342479319056 · Noura 7401593626448363521.
  • Window: 2026-05-01 → 2026-06-16 (for stable creative reads).
  • Filter: creatives with ≥80 SAR spend (≥40 in detail table).
  • Fields: spend, clicks, ctr, cpc, total_landing_page_view, play_duration_2s/6s, web_event_add_to_cart, initiate_checkout, complete_payment, total_complete_payment_rate.

Derivations & cross-checks

  • LPV/100 = LPV ÷ spend × 100 · Intent/100 = (ATC+IC) ÷ spend × 100.
  • Thumbstop = 2s-views ÷ impressions (image/OW creatives = 0 by format).
  • Hero product: cross-referenced to Salla top-sellers (Asayel 358u; Zain #54 53% of sales + top cart-abandon).
  • Feeder's strategic value: the day-split natural experiment (Sales ROAS 8.2 vs 4.4 with feeder ON vs OFF, May).
  • Caveat: ad_name = caption/theme (may map to >1 video). Confirm the exact video in-account by name before launch.
Creative selection is data-driven and reproducible from the fields above. Settings labels = English TikTok UI; ad captions = Arabic (Saudi audience). · Currency SAR.
