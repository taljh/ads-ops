The Decision (and why I stand behind it)

✅ DECISION: Build a lean, always-on Feeder / Traffic campaign under each Sales campaign — running the proven winning creative on the hero product, at a small fixed budget.

This reverses my earlier "kill the traffic campaign" call. I judged the old traffic campaign on its direct ROAS (0 purchases) — wrong lens. The right lens is its assist/halo effect on the Sales campaign, which the data proves is large and consistent.

Evidence (same Sales engine «Winners CBO / #54», days Traffic ON vs OFF):

Window (confound-controlled)Traffic ONTraffic OFFLift on Sales campaign
May only (fully matured — cleanest)ROAS 8.20 · CPA 49 · 3.4 ord/dayROAS 4.41 · CPA 88 · 2.5 ord/dayROAS +86% · CPA −44%
Matured (May1–Jun11)ROAS 7.04 · CPA 57ROAS 4.18 · CPA 95ROAS +68% · CPA −40%
Around Jun-7 shutoffROAS 5.91 · CPA 68ROAS 3.00 · CPA 148ROAS +97%

Robust across 3 independent cuts. The OFF period even included the payroll-peak days (26→30) that should have favored it — and still lost. Mechanism: cheap broad reach (CPM ~1.3, CTR ~8%) feeds the pixel with qualified visits + builds a warm/aware pool → the Conversion campaign's algorithm finds buyers faster (lower CPA) and the halo lifts direct sales (Salla: Direct = 64% of revenue). Caveat: still correlational → we run a clean ON/OFF test (Section 6) to confirm and decide keep-vs-kill.

Measurement & clean ON/OFF test (how I'll prove it)

Phase 1 — Run + read against the existing baseline (now → Jun 30)

Turn the feeder ON during the month-end push (evidence says it helps; cost is tiny). I compare the Sales campaign's CPA/ROAS in the feeder-ON window vs the recent feeder-OFF baseline we already have:

  • Baseline (feeder OFF, Jun 8–16): Winners CBO ≈ ROAS 3.0 / CPA 148 (Zain).
  • Target with feeder ON: ROAS back toward 5–7 / CPA ≤ 80.

Phase 2 — Clean A/B (early July, post-peak)

To remove timing confounds, a deliberate switch test while the Sales campaign is unchanged:

  • Week ON (feeder live) vs Week OFF (feeder paused).
  • Metric = Sales-campaign CPA & ROAS + blended (Salla) delta.
  • Decision rule: if ON beats OFF by ≥15% on CPA → keep & scale feeder; if no difference → kill it (it was a coincidence); if worse → kill.
